About BloodNet

Connecting heroes with those in need.

Our Mission

We built BloodNet with a singular, vital goal: to eliminate blood shortages by creating a seamless, geo-aware network connecting willing donors with hospitals and patients in real-time.

In critical emergencies, time is the difference between life and death. Our platform uses advanced location-matching to instantly alert nearby eligible donors when an emergency arises.

100% Free Service

We never charge for matching donors with patients.
