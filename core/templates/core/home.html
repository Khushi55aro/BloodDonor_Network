How It Works

Simple 4-step workflow to connect blood seekers with nearby donors.

1. Donor Register

Donor registers with blood group, location coordinates, and last donation date.

2. Eligibility Check

System calculates donor eligibility based on a 90-day cooldown period.

3. Recipient Request

Recipient posts a blood request with required blood group and location.

4. Geo-Match & Notify

Haversine formula finds nearest compatible eligible donors and notifies them.
